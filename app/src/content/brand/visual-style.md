# Visual Style: TheFeed

## Colors
- **Primary:** #2563EB - Electric blue, tech-forward trust signal
- **Secondary:** #0F172A - Deep slate, dark backgrounds
- **Accent:** #F97316 - Warm orange, energy and approachability
- **Surface:** #1E293B - Card/panel backgrounds
- **Muted:** #94A3B8 - Secondary text, borders

## Style
- **Primary style:** photography + screenshots
- **Quality:** editorial, authentic
- Real screens, real tools, workspace aesthetics
- Dark mode default with high contrast accents

## Mood
vibrant, approachable, builder-workshop, energetic, authentic

## Photography Style
- Real tool screenshots and workspace shots
- Behind-the-screen builder aesthetics
- No stock photos, no generic AI art
- Screen recordings and tool demos preferred

## Typography
- Headlines: Bold, tight tracking, high impact
- Body: Clean sans-serif, optimized for scanning
- Code: Monospace with syntax highlighting

## Keywords
- builder workshop
- real tools real screens
- dark mode tech
- energetic approachable
- signal over noise

## Avoid
- Corporate blue gradients
- Generic AI-generated hero images
- Sterile minimalism with no personality
- Stock photos of people pointing at screens
- Overly polished/glossy aesthetic

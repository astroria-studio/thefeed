# Brand Voice: TheFeed

## Identity
- **Name:** TheFeed
- **Tagline:** AI & tech for people who build things
- **USP:** Curated, opinionated AI/tech coverage with a maker-first lens

## Tone
Playful + friendly — like a smart builder friend sharing finds over coffee.

## Style Guidelines
- Conversational, direct, opinionated
- Specific over vague — cite tools tested, revenue numbers, real results
- Anti-hype — call out noise, respect the audience's intelligence
- Short sentences. Scannable. Signal-dense.
- OK to use casual language, contractions, rhetorical questions

## Voice Examples
- YES: "We tested 8 AI code editors for a week. Here's what actually shipped."
- YES: "This tool costs $20/mo and saved me 6 hours. That's the whole review."
- NO: "AI is revolutionizing the way we build software solutions."
- NO: "Top 50 AI tools you NEED to check out right now!"

## Vocabulary
- Use: ship, build, maker, workflow, stack, signal, noise, tested, honest
- Avoid: revolutionary, game-changing, disruptive, synergy, leverage, ecosystem (unironically)

## Do
- Share honest takes including negative ones
- Say "this is noise" when something is noise
- Use numbers and specifics over adjectives
- Write like you're texting a smart friend, not pitching a VC

## Don't
- Press release language
- "AI will change everything" hype
- Generic listicles without editorial judgment
- Corporate tone or buzzword stacking
- Clickbait headlines that overpromise

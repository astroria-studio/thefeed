# Audience Profiles: TheFeed

## Primary: Indie Makers & Solopreneurs

### Who
Solo developers, bootstrapped SaaS founders, creator-entrepreneurs, side-project builders. Technical or semi-technical. Building real products, not just following trends.

### Demographics
- Age: 25-45
- Location: Global, English-speaking
- Income: Variable — side hustlers to $10K+ MRR founders
- Platforms: X/Twitter, Indie Hackers, HN, Discord, YouTube

### Pain Points
- **Tool fatigue:** AI tool landscape explodes weekly, impossible to evaluate everything
- **Hype-reality gap:** Most AI coverage doesn't match lived building experience
- **Time poverty:** Bandwidth-constrained, need signal not volume
- **Technical Cliff:** Vibe coding gets to prototype, production is a different beast
- **Content creation bottleneck:** 2-4 hours per content piece, need workflows not tools lists

### Goals
- Ship products faster with the right AI tools
- Stay informed without drowning in noise
- Find tested, honest tool recommendations
- Learn real workflows from people actually building
- Optimize AI spend (right model for right task)

### Content Preferences
- Short, scannable discovery formats
- Willing to go deep on topics they invest in
- Strong preference for opinionated voices over aggregators
- Podcast + newsletter combo is dominant media diet
- Short video for quick tool demos

## Secondary: Non-Technical Founders

### Who
Building with no-code/low-code AI tools (Lovable, Bolt, v0). Entrepreneurs who think in products, not syntax.

### Pain Points
- Need workflow guidance, not code tutorials
- Struggle bridging prototype to production
- Overwhelmed by technical jargon in AI content

### Goals
- Build and launch without hiring a dev team
- Understand enough tech to make smart decisions
- Find tools that match their skill level

Welcome to TheFeed! This is your first post.

## Getting Started

TheFeed is a modern content platform built with Astro and deployed on Cloudflare. It supports multiple content formats:

- **Articles** - Long-form written content with MDX support
- **Videos** - Embedded videos from YouTube or self-hosted
- **Audio** - Podcasts and audio content
- **Quotes** - Memorable quotes and insights
- **Galleries** - Image collections

## Next Steps

1. Customize your `thefeed.config.ts` with your site details
2. Update the i18n strings in `app/src/i18n/en.json`
3. Add your own content to the `content/` directories
4. Deploy to Cloudflare Pages

Happy publishing!
